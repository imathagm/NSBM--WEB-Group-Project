Inter-university and club tournament placements

  • USports 2023: boys’ champions; girls’ runners-up
  • USports 2022: male champions; female second runners-up
  • GSF 2024: runners-up; ICBT Smash Down 2024 (girls): second runners-up
  • ICBT Smash Down 2023 (girls): second runners-up

TABLE TENNIS · USPORTS 2022

Mixed-team champions

NSBM’s mixed team were champions, with the men’s and women’s teams finishing second at USports 2022.

NETBALL · UNIVERSITY COMPETITIONS

Inter-university podium finishes

NSBM finished second at USports 2022, runners-up at the KDU Leaders Trophy in 2017 and 2018, and second runners-up at CIMA 2017.

SWIMMING · 2017

Interuniversity Swimming Championship

NSBM won the Interuniversity Swimming Championship / USPORTS 2017.
